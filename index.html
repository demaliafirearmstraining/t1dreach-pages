Delete your T1DReach account

T1DReach is a peer-support community app for people living with Type 1 diabetes and their caregivers. You can delete your account and data at any time.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open T1DReach and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab (or Settings).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't sign in or no longer have the app, email T1DeMalia@gmail.com with the subject "T1DReach account deletion request", from the email address on your account. We confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deleted: your sign-in account, profile (including T1D details and photo), approximate location, posts, comments, reactions, help and Beacon requests and responses, Supply Locker posts, messages you sent, conversation memberships, blocks, notifications, push tokens, and alert preferences.

Kept: records of moderation actions taken against content that broke our Community Guidelines may be kept for up to 1 year for safety purposes. Messages other members sent to you stay in their own conversation history.

T1DReach is peer support, not medical or emergency care. In an emergency, call 911.
